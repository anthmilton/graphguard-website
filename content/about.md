## Our Story

Developed by anglers, field-tested on the water, and launched to solve a problem we faced every day: scratched screens and unbearable glare. 

Marine electronics are a major investment. We realized that premium glass protectors were often too expensive and prone to shattering, while cheap film protectors simply didn't last a full season. GraphGuard is the sweet spot—professional-grade protection that won't break the bank.

## Why We Exist

We believe in protecting your investment with precision. Our protectors are field-tested through full fishing seasons, ensuring they stand up to the harshest marine environments.
