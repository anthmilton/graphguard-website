### Tips

**What You'll Need:**
- Microfiber cloth
- Screen cleaning solution or alcohol prep pad
- Included squeegee card

**Pro Tips:**
- Install in a dust-free environment (indoors is best).
- Wash your hands before starting to avoid fingerprints on the adhesive side.
- If a bubble remains, gently push it toward the nearest edge.
